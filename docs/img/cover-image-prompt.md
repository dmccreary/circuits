# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

**Circuits 1** is a textbook about analog electrical circuits and signal
analysis, with audio systems used as a unifying real-world theme — from
DC fundamentals through AC phasors, RLC resonance, Fourier series, and
op-amp filter design. The intended audience is first- and second-year
college students majoring in electrical engineering or computer science.
The visual tone should be **modern, scientific, and precise, with a warm
"audio-equipment" feel** — technical but inviting, not sterile.

## Title

Place **Circuits 1** in the center of the image, in a clean, highly
legible geometric sans-serif font (think the title treatment on a
modern engineering textbook or a high-end audio product). Use a
light/white font color with a subtle dark scrim or soft drop shadow
behind it so it stays readable against the busy montage background.
Below the main title, set a smaller tagline reading
"**An Interactive Textbook — Analog & Signal Analysis**" in a lighter
weight. Keep both lines short enough to render at large size and leave
generous negative space immediately behind the text.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each
rendered in a consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the
composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles.
Spread them around the frame — do not cluster them on one side — and
keep the area directly behind the title relatively clean:

- **Oscilloscope with overlapping sine waves** — a dark screen with a
  faint grid and three sine-wave traces (fundamental plus harmonics) in
  cyan, amber, and violet, suggesting Fourier content of an audio signal.
- **Resistor with color bands** — a classic axial through-hole resistor
  shown large and in sharp detail, with recognizable red/orange/brown
  color bands and silver tolerance band.
- **Op-amp schematic symbol** — the classic triangle op-amp with `+` and
  `−` inputs, an output arrow, and a feedback resistor loop, drawn in
  clean schematic line style.
- **Parallel-plate capacitor charging curve** — an exponential
  `1 − e^(−t/RC)` curve rising to a dashed asymptote, with the time
  constant τ marked on the horizontal axis.
- **RLC resonant tank** — a small schematic showing an inductor coil in
  parallel with a capacitor, with a small resonance peak curve nearby.
- **Bode plot** — a magnitude-vs-frequency plot on log axes showing a
  low-pass response with a visible −3 dB corner frequency marker.
- **Phasor diagram on the complex plane** — a rotating vector arrow at
  an angle in the first quadrant, with faint real/imaginary axes and a
  small `jω` label, representing AC steady-state analysis.
- **Audio speaker emitting sound waves** — a stylized cone speaker with
  concentric arcs radiating outward, tying the montage back to the
  book's audio-circuits theme.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, **Sparky the Lightbulb**, in the **lower-left
corner** of the image. Sparky should be clearly visible but sized so he
does not overlap the title text or crowd the montage elements above him.
Give him a small, soft elliptical warm-golden ground glow beneath him so
he reads as "standing on" the lower-left corner rather than floating in
the middle of a montage panel.

Sparky is a friendly anthropomorphic incandescent light bulb mascot in a
warm, polished cartoon style with soft cel-shading and a gentle outer
glow:

- Classic A-shape incandescent bulb forming the head and body as one
  continuous warm golden-yellow glass form, with bright white highlights
  on the upper-left curve.
- Face centered on the bulb: two large round eyes with bright blue
  irises, crisp black pupils, and a single white catch-light; small soft
  black eyebrows; two soft pink circular blush marks on the cheeks.
- Edison screw base below the glass: blue insulator ring, silver/gray
  threaded metal section, small dark contact tip.
- Two short tubular yellow cartoon arms with white four-fingered gloves
  and a yellow cuff band.
- In this cover pose, Sparky is in a welcoming stance — one hand raised
  in a friendly wave, grinning warmly toward the reader, eyes bright and
  wide. Pose feels energetic and inviting, like a host introducing the
  course.

A reference pose is available at `docs/img/mascots/welcome.png` — match
that character design exactly for color, proportions, and style.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: **flat vector with subtle gradients and soft inner
  glow**, like a modern technical infographic. Apply the same style to
  every montage element so the composition reads as one image.
- Color palette: **deep indigo / dark purple background (#1a1033 to
  #2d1b69) with amber-gold accents (#D4A017, #FFD54F) and cyan highlights
  (#7DD3FC)** — matches the Material "deep purple + amber" theme of the
  textbook site. Sparky's warm golden glow should harmonize with the
  amber accents.
- Lighting / mood: **bright, optimistic, and inviting**, with a subtle
  radial glow behind the title and behind Sparky. Overall feel is a
  "lit-from-within" circuit board at night.
- Composition:
  - Title centered horizontally, slightly above vertical center.
  - Montage elements arranged in a loose ring around the title, spread
    across all four quadrants but **not** in the lower-left corner (that
    space is reserved for Sparky) and **not** directly behind the title.
  - Sparky anchored in the lower-left corner, approximately 18–22% of the
    image height, with a small warm ground glow beneath him.
  - Generous negative space immediately behind the title text and
    immediately above Sparky so neither feels crowded.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
- Do not place any montage element on top of Sparky or on top of the
  title text.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs
  unrelated to Sparky, people pointing at whiteboards).
- Avoid photorealistic human faces.
- Do not use a white, black, or checkered background behind Sparky — he
  must integrate with the dark-indigo circuit scene.
- Do not shrink the title to fit; simplify the background directly behind
  it instead.
